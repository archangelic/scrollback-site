Accessibility

Scrollback is built to work with the accessibility features in iOS, iPadOS and macOS.

VoiceOver

  • Each message reads as one item: who sent it, what it says, what it replies to, its reactions and its time.
  • Replying, reacting, copying, deleting, reporting and opening links are VoiceOver actions on each message.
  • New mentions and private messages in the open conversation are read out as they arrive. Turn this off in Settings → Conversations.
  • Every button and field is labelled, and conversations say how many unread messages and mentions they have.

Voice Control

Every control can be spoken by name. Channels also answer to their name without the #.

Text and reading

  • Chat text follows the system text size, up to the largest accessibility sizes, or stays at a size you set.
  • Fonts chosen for easier reading: Atkinson Hyperlegible, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, Lexend and OpenDyslexic.
  • Adjustable line spacing, and Bold Text is followed.

Color, contrast and motion

  • Light and dark appearance, following the system or set in the app.
  • Nickname colors are adjusted for readable contrast in both, and Increase Contrast strengthens secondary text.
  • With Differentiate Without Color, links are underlined, mentions are marked with a bar, and connection status uses symbols as well as color.
  • Reduce Motion removes sliding and scaling animations.

Keyboard

With a hardware keyboard, Return sends, Shift-Return adds a line, Tab completes nicknames, Up and Down recall sent messages, and Option-Up and Option-Down move between conversations.

Feedback

If something in Scrollback doesn't work with the way you use your device, email scrollback@archangelic.space. Accessibility problems are fixed first.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Scrollback is made by Michael Hancock, who is responsible for this policy. Scrollback doesn't collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own, so nothing you do in the app is sent to its developer. This policy describes what the app sends, and where, when you use it.

IRC servers and bouncers

Scrollback connects only to the IRC servers and bouncers you add. It sends them what IRC needs: your nickname, your username and real name, your login, and the messages you send. Those servers, and the people in your channels, can see your messages and your IP address. Each network has its own operators and policies, which Scrollback doesn't control.

Messages and settings are stored on your device. Passwords are stored in the device's keychain.

iCloud sync

With iCloud Sync on (Settings → Sync), your networks, highlight words, and image preview and upload settings are stored in your iCloud account. Your passwords are stored in iCloud Keychain. Apple holds this data under your Apple Account; Michael Hancock can't see it. Messages aren't synced. Turn iCloud Sync off to stop syncing.

Image previews

Image previews are off until you turn them on. When they're on, Scrollback downloads images linked in messages from the sites you allow, which tells those sites your IP address and when you viewed the message. Scrollback only loads secure (HTTPS) links, sends no cookies, and never loads addresses on your local network.

Uploads

When you attach a photo or file, Scrollback uploads it to your bouncer's file host, or to a file host you set up in Settings. Anyone with the link can open the file. Photos are resized, and their location and other metadata are removed before upload.

Push notifications from ZNC

If you set up push notifications for a ZNC bouncer, your bouncer sends notifications through the service you choose, ntfy or Pushover, using the details you enter. Scrollback only sends those settings to your bouncer.

Notifications and Shortcuts

Notifications are created on your device. Shortcuts read the names of your conversations and their latest messages from storage on your device.

Deleting your data

Delete a network in Scrollback to remove its settings, password and messages from your device, and from iCloud when sync is on. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Messages you've sent stay on the IRC networks and bouncers that received them; ask their operators to remove them.

Children

Scrollback isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page, with the date at the top updated.

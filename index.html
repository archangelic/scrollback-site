IRC that feels brand new

Scrollback is an IRC client for iPhone and iPad, built on modern IRCv3. The history you missed is waiting when you open it, and replies, reactions and typing work wherever your server supports them.

Features

Everything modern IRC can do, in your pocket

Scrollback speaks the IRCv3 extensions that servers like Ergo, soju and InspIRCd offer, and works with any IRC network.

  • History that waits for you

    Messages you missed load when you open a conversation, and read positions stay in step with your other clients.

  • Replies and reactions

    Answer a message directly, react with any emoji, and see who's typing, on servers that support them.

  • Messages with line breaks

    Send code and lists as one message where the server takes multiline messages, and as separate lines where it doesn't.

  • Bouncers, built in

    All of a soju bouncer's networks from one login. ZNC logins, playback, and push notifications through ntfy or Pushover.

  • Photos and previews

    Share photos through your bouncer's file host, shrunk and with their location removed. Image previews are off until you turn them on.

  • Secure by default

    TLS with certificate pinning for self-signed servers, SASL logins, and servers' strict transport policies honored.

  • Accessible throughout

    VoiceOver labels and actions everywhere, Dynamic Type, reading fonts, and full keyboard control on iPad.

  • Shortcuts and Focus

    Send messages, count mentions and mark everything read from Shortcuts, and choose which networks may notify during a Focus.

  • Tools for staying safe

    Report a message to a channel's operators or the network's staff, and ignore anyone, hiding everything they send.

Networks

Every network, one list

Add a server, a bouncer, or a whole soju account, and every conversation lands in one list you can read at a glance.

  • Unread counts and mentions on every channel and private message.
  • A soju bouncer's networks appear by themselves, and stay in step as you add more.
  • iCloud sync brings your networks and passwords to your other devices.
  • Setup links share a network's settings with friends, without the password.
Search

Find anything anyone said

Search every conversation at once, or just one. Narrow it down with from: and in:, then jump straight to the message.

  • Your soju bouncer's stored history is searched too, not just what's on this device.
  • Mentions gathers every message that names you or your highlight words.
Reading

Made for reading

Pick a font designed for dyslexia or low vision, set the size and spacing, and watch a live preview of the conversation as you choose.

  • Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend and OpenDyslexic, plus monospaced fonts for code.
  • Text that follows your system size, or stays where you set it.
  • VoiceOver reads out mentions as they arrive in the open conversation.
iPad

Room for the whole conversation

Your networks stay beside the conversation on iPad, with keyboard shortcuts for moving between them.

Privacy

Your conversations are yours

Scrollback connects only to the servers you add. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it sends, and where.

No accounts

There's nothing to sign up for. Add a network and start talking.

No analytics or ads

Nothing you do in the app is sent to its developer.

No servers of its own

Messages travel between you and your IRC servers, and stay on your device.

Passwords in the Keychain

Logins live in the keychain, or iCloud Keychain when you turn on sync. Setup links never carry them.

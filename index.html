Scrollback

An IRC client for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built on modern IRCv3.

Scrollback connects to IRC networks and to bouncers such as soju and ZNC. It loads the history you missed, keeps read positions in step with your other clients, and supports replies, reactions, typing indicators and messages with line breaks wherever the server does.

Features

  • Message history and read sync, through IRCv3 chat history and read markers.
  • All of a soju bouncer's networks from one login.
  • Search across every conversation, including a soju bouncer's stored history.
  • Uploads to your bouncer's file host, with photos shrunk and their location removed.
  • Image previews, off until you turn them on, from the sites you choose.
  • iCloud sync of your networks and passwords.
  • Widgets, Shortcuts and Focus filters.
  • Fonts made for easier reading, VoiceOver support and full keyboard control.

Scrollback has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it sends where.

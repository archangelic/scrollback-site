Nothing you missed is lost

History from your bouncer

Scrollback asks your bouncer or server for exactly what happened while your phone was asleep, through IRCv3 chat history or ZNC playback, and fills the gap without duplicates.

Read on one device, read everywhere

Read markers keep your place in step with your other clients, so you don't scroll past the same messages on your laptop.

Notifications through ZNC

Scrollback sets up ZNC's push module for you, sending mentions and private messages to ntfy or Pushover while the app is closed. Each one opens the right conversation.

Everything a daily IRC client needs

  • Every soju network, one login

    Add your soju bouncer once and each of its networks shows up in the sidebar.

  • Search

    Find a message across every conversation, including history stored on your soju bouncer.

  • Photos and files

    Upload to your bouncer's file host or one you set up. Photos are shrunk and their location removed first.

  • Image previews you control

    Off until you turn them on, then only from the sites you choose.

  • iCloud sync

    Your networks follow you between iPhone, iPad and Mac. Passwords stay in iCloud Keychain.

  • Shortcuts and Focus

    Send messages and open conversations from Shortcuts, and choose which networks can notify you during a Focus.

  • Replies, reactions and more

    Threaded replies, emoji reactions, typing indicators and messages with line breaks, wherever the server supports them.

  • Made for keyboards

    Nickname completion, message recall and shortcuts to move between conversations on iPad and Mac.

Built for VoiceOver, Voice Control and large text

Every message reads as one item, with reply, react and copy as VoiceOver actions. Chat text follows the system text size up to the largest accessibility sizes. Nickname colors keep readable contrast in light and dark, and nothing depends on color alone.

Questions

Does it work with ZNC?

Yes. Choose ZNC when you add a network and enter your ZNC user, network and password. Scrollback supports ZNC's playback and self-message modules and can set up its push module.

Does it work with soju?

Yes. Sign in once with SASL and all of the bouncer's networks appear, with its stored history and file host.

Do I need a bouncer?

No. Scrollback connects to any IRC network directly. A bouncer keeps you in your channels while your phone is asleep, so history is waiting when you come back.

Does it stay connected in the background?

iPhone and iPad pause apps soon after you leave them. Scrollback uses the time it's given, then reconnects and fills in what you missed when you return.

What does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, and Mac computers with Apple silicon.

Is it a subscription?

No. Try everything free for 14 days, then unlock it with one purchase that covers iPhone, iPad and Mac and can be shared with your family.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics or servers of its own. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent where.

Community Guidelines

Everyone agrees to these the first time they open Scrollback. You can read them again in Settings → Community Guidelines.

Scrollback connects you to IRC networks, where you can talk with people all over the world. Those networks are run by their own volunteers and operators, not by Scrollback.

By using Scrollback, you agree to:

  • Not post anything abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, threatening or illegal.
  • Not harass, spam or impersonate other people.
  • Follow the rules of each network and channel you join.

There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Each network's staff and channel operators can remove or ban people who break their rules.

If someone bothers you

  • Ignore them. Touch and hold their message, then choose Ignore. Everything they say, their private messages and their notifications are hidden from then on, and what they already said is removed. You can also type /ignore nick.
  • Report them. Touch and hold their message, then choose Report. Scrollback sends a copy of the message to one of the channel's operators, who can remove the person from the channel, or asks the network for its staff, who can ban them from the network.

Contact

To report a problem with Scrollback itself, email scrollback@archangelic.space.

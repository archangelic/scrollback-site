Support

Email scrollback@archangelic.space for help, bug reports and feature requests.

Include your device, its iOS or macOS version, the version of Scrollback (Settings → About), and the server or bouncer you're connecting to. For a connection problem, copy the Raw Log from the network's menu; it leaves out your passwords.

Common questions

How do I connect to my ZNC bouncer?

Add a network with your bouncer's address, then choose ZNC under Authentication and enter your ZNC username, network and password.

How do I use my soju bouncer?

Add your bouncer as one network, with SASL under Authentication. Each of the bouncer's networks then appears in the sidebar on its own.

My server uses a self-signed certificate.

When the certificate isn't trusted, Scrollback shows its fingerprint. Check it against your server's, then choose Trust This Certificate.

Why don't images show?

Image previews are off until you turn them on in Settings → Image Previews, and then only show from the sites you allow.

How do I report someone?

Hold a message and choose Report. IRC networks are run by their own staff, so reports go to the channel's operators or the network's staff. You can also ignore someone to hide everything they say. The Community Guidelines explain both.
